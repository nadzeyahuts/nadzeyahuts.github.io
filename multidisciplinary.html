FAQ

Questions hiring managers ask.

What is a multidisciplinary designer?

A multidisciplinary designer works across several design areas at once: product UI, brand identity, marketing graphics, motion and print. For a startup or a small marketing team it means one person keeps every touchpoint in one visual language.

What do you design besides UX/UI?

Logos and brand guides, mascots, presentation templates and sales decks, one-pagers and PDF reports, ad banner sets in standard IAB sizes, social media posts and LinkedIn carousels, animated posts, infographics and charts, editorial hero illustrations, landing pages, brochures and flyers.

Which tools do you use?

Figma for layout, brand systems and templates; Photoshop and Illustrator for images, retouching and vector work; AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, Nano Banana and Figma AI to speed up concepts, image cleanup and production.

Can you handle high-volume production?

Yes. The report campaign above is one master banner adapted to 11 sizes, and at Savii Digital I produced 490 banners in 43 campaign packages. I build templates and repeatable workflows so volume does not cost quality.

Which industries does this work cover?

Mostly fintech, banking software, Web3 and AI products: 4IRE, ABLE Platform and RNDpoint, Genius Radar, DeepSage, Syntra, Rumata and decio.ai.
